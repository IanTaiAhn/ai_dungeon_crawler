# The Volcanic Heart

Beneath the frozen cavern - beneath even the black ice the Frostwardens
raised - the crown finally explains itself. Lift it from its pillar and
the cold it carries all its life wakes something older: a crack splits
the cavern floor and breathes heat up out of the dark, a passage no
Frostwarden record ever mentions, down into rock the frost never
reached.

No ledger, Warden or Frostwarden, describes what's down there. Whatever
sealed it did so long before either order existed.

## The Ashborn

Two guardians hold the passage down: a magma hound loosed in the upper
passage, and an ember wraith coiled deeper in the volcanic depths below
it, closer to the heart. Neither is territorial the way the guard room's
goblin is - they don't hold ground, they follow whoever's carrying the
crown, drawn to the cold radiating off it the way moths follow a flame
in reverse.

## The Hollow

At the bottom, past both guardians, a ledge overlooks a lake of lava,
and at its center sits a hollow worn to the exact shape of a crown - not
carved, not built, simply shaped by something standing there so long the
stone gave way to it. Every account that gets this far agrees the crown
belongs there, not on a pillar of ice.

Set the frostbound crown into the hollow and the volcanic heart stops
being a ruin at all: the lava dims and skins over, cracks knit shut, and
green takes the chamber the way it must have looked before whatever
happened here happened. Nothing that reaches the hollow and sets the
crown down has ever needed to fight its way back out.
