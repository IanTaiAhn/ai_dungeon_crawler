# Past significant events

- **The Ashfall**: the night the Sunken Outpost's garrison was
  overrun. The cause was never confirmed - some records blame a goblin
  incursion, others a fire that started in the guard room's stores and
  spread before anyone could respond.
- **The Sealing**: shortly before the Ashfall, the Paymaster sealed the
  ancient amulet in the treasure room rather than let it fall into
  raiding hands.
- **Since then**: no successful retrieval of the amulet has ever been
  recorded. Adventuring parties who enter tend to stop at the guard
  room's goblin sentry and turn back.
