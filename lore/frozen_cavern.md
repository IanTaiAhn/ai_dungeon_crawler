# The Frozen Cavern

Beneath the treasure room, sealed behind a wall the garrison never
mapped, lies a frozen cavern that predates the Sunken Outpost itself.
Black ice climbs its walls, and cold radiates up through the treasure
room's floor stones even now - guards on watch there used to joke that
the pedestal itself was carved from a single block of the cavern's ice,
hauled up before anyone remembered why.

The amulet the Paymaster sealed away wasn't the relic worth guarding.
It was the key. Old Warden ledgers describe it as "ceremonial, do not
requisition" because whoever wrote that entry knew exactly what it
opened and wanted no one asking. Brought close to the frozen cavern's
sealed door, the amulet resonates, and the ice parts.

## The Frostwardens

Long before the Wardens of the Pass held the outpost above, an older
order - remembered only as the Frostwardens - raised crystal golems out
of the black ice to guard what the cavern holds: a crown, bound in
frost, said to have once granted its wearer command over the passage
itself. No account records a Frostwarden ever leaving the cavern; the
golems are the only guardians anyone has actually seen.

## The Frostbound Crown

Set into a pillar of ice at the cavern's heart. Unlike the amulet, no
one disputes what it is - every surviving fragment of Frostwarden
record calls it the true seat of authority over the outpost, the
amulet only ever a copy carried by whoever held the Paymaster's rank.
Recovering it is the only ending to this ruin anyone has ever
recorded reaching.
