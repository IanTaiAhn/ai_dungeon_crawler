# Notable figures

## The Guard Room Goblin
No name is recorded for it - just "the goblin in the guard room," the
way locals describe it. It's territorial rather than organized: it
will fight to hold the room, but has never been seen pursuing anyone
who retreats down the entrance passage.

## The Paymaster
An unnamed Warden officer, last seen sealing the treasure room before
the Ashfall. Garrison ledgers (long since rotted) reportedly recorded
the amulet as "keep-issue, ceremonial, do not requisition" - suggesting
it was symbolic of command rather than magical, though rumors disagree.
