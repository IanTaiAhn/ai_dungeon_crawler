# The Sunken Outpost

Long ago, the Sunken Outpost was a border keep built to watch the low
passes into the valley. It fell during the Ashfall, when the garrison
was overrun in a single night and the keep's lower halls flooded with
ash and rain. Nothing was rebuilt. The stone entrance still stands,
half-swallowed by moss, and locals say lights are sometimes seen
moving behind the old guard room's arrow-slits after dark.

The outpost has three known chambers: the entrance hall, the guard
room, and - beyond a door no one has opened in a generation - the
treasure room, where the garrison's paymaster is said to have sealed
the keep's most valuable relic before the walls came down.
