# Factions

## The Wardens of the Pass
The garrison that once held the Sunken Outpost. Disbanded after the
Ashfall, but a handful of descendants still consider the amulet
described below theirs by right, and occasionally hire adventurers to
retrieve it rather than risk the ruins themselves.

## The Green Marches
A loose confederation of goblin clans that moved into the lowland
ruins after the Wardens fell. They don't hold territory so much as
squat in it - a lone goblin sentry in an old guard room is typical: not
an army, just someone who found a defensible room and never left.
