# The Ember Ward

Old Warden ledgers hedge on what the amulet actually is - "ceremonial,
do not requisition" - but the hedge was itself the tell. What the
ledgers don't record: the amulet carries a dormant ember ward, a
working of flame bound into the metal by whoever guarded the outpost
before the Wardens ever held it. Most who carried it never knew. It
answers only to someone who has already proven a claim to what lies
below the treasure room - which is to say, no one has answered it in
generations.

## Flame against ice

The frozen cavern's guardian was never raised to withstand fire. Black
ice sheds a blade's edge easily enough, but the Frostwardens who bound
the crystal golem apparently never anticipated an intruder carrying a
working ember ward of their own. A golem struck by real flame doesn't
just take damage - it cracks visibly, ice sloughing off in sheets.
Whether that was an oversight, or the Frostwardens simply never
expected the ward and the golem to meet the same intruder, isn't
recorded.
